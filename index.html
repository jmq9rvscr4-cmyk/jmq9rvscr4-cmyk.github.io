A brief introduction

I’m interested in the gap between good enough and actually right.

My work began in veterinary medicine, where details matter and clear thinking has real consequences. That same instinct follows me everywhere: learn the system, notice what others miss, and leave things better than I found them.

This site is a home for the work, questions, and side projects that don’t fit neatly into a résumé.
